Everything about how the store behaves, on one long page. The list down the left
jumps you to each section.

<Warning>
  **Nothing saves until you press Save Changes.** The top of the page tells you
  whether you have unsaved changes. Leaving the page without saving loses your
  edits.
</Warning>

## Store Information

The core details, used on invoices and around the console.

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Store name** | Your business name. |
| **Support email** | Where customer replies should reach you. |
| **Phone** | Your business number. |
| **GSTIN** | Optional. Fill it in once you have one and it appears on invoices. |

## Brand & Contact

What customers see across the storefront. Leave any field blank to fall back to
the built-in default — the default is shown in grey so you can see what you'd
get.

- **Descriptor** — the line under your name, e.g. *Artificial Bridal Jewellery*
- **WhatsApp number** — with country code; this is what the WhatsApp buttons dial
- **Tagline** — your one-line pitch
- **Address line**, **City**, **State**
- **Visiting note** — anything customers should know before turning up
- **Opening hours** — a short form and a full form
- **Hours note** — e.g. *Closed on festival days*

## Shipping & Payments

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Free shipping above (₹)** | Baskets at or above this ship free. |
| **Flat shipping rate (₹)** | What everyone below that threshold pays. |
| **Cash on Delivery** | Allow COD across India. |
| **Razorpay Live Mode** | Online card and UPI payments — **coming soon**, not yet available. |

<Tip>
  The free-shipping threshold is the most powerful number on this page. Set it a
  little above your average basket and customers add one more piece to reach it.
</Tip>

## Returns

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Return window after delivery (days)** | How long customers have to request a return. **Setting it to 0 turns returns off entirely.** |
| **Return shipping is paid by** | *Customer pays*, *Store pays*, or *Store pays only for defective / wrong items*. |

Customers see a **Request return** option on delivered orders inside that
window. The shipping-payer choice only changes the note they're shown — refunds
are always UPI payments you make yourself and record in
[Returns](/selling/returns).

## Announcement Banner

The thin strip along the very top of your storefront. **Changes apply live** —
saved immediately, no waiting.

Switch it on with **Enable announcement banner**, then fill in:

- **Message 1** and **Message 2** — e.g. *"Free shipping over ₹999"*,
  *"Cash on Delivery available"*
- **Offer label** and **Offer highlight** — e.g. *"Festive offer:"* and
  *"FLAT 20% OFF"*
- **Promo code** — the code customers should use, e.g. `BRIDE20`

<Note>
  If you advertise a code here, make sure that
  [coupon](/catalogue/coupons) is actually **Active**. A banner promising a code
  that doesn't work costs you the sale and the trust.
</Note>

## Homepage Hero

The photo in the framed card beside your homepage headline. Upload one, or
**Replace photo** to swap it.

<Note>
  Unlike the banner, **hero uploads apply on Save** — press Save Changes after
  uploading.
</Note>

## Homepage Promo Block

The large festive offer block further down the homepage. Switch it on with
**Enable homepage promo block**, then set:

- **Eyebrow** — small text above, e.g. *"Festive Season Offer"*
- **Headline** — the big line, e.g. *"Flat 20% off the entire bridal range"*
- **Button label** — e.g. *"Shop the Sale"*
- **Promo code** and **Note** — e.g. `BRIDE20`, *"at checkout. Limited period."*

Changes apply live.

<Warning>
  **Turn the promo block off when the offer ends.** A Diwali block still sitting
  there in February makes the whole shop look abandoned.
</Warning>

## Notifications

Alerts on your phone or computer for new orders, cancellations, messages and
reviews.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Press 'Enable on this device'">
    Your browser will ask permission. Say yes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press 'Send a test'">
    Confirms it actually works before you rely on it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  This is **per device**. Enabling it on your laptop doesn't enable it on your
  phone — turn it on separately wherever you want alerts. The phone is usually
  the one that matters.
</Note>

## Storage

Housekeeping for uploaded photos. **Clean up unused images** removes images no
longer attached to any product or category, freeing up space.

Safe to run whenever. It only removes what nothing points at.

## Store status

At the top of the page: **Live & accepting orders**. That's your confirmation
the storefront is up and taking business.
