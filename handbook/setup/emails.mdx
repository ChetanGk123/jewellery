Your store emails customers by itself — when an order arrives, when it ships,
when a refund is paid. This screen is where you change what those emails say.

<Note>
  You don't send anything from here. These are the automatic emails; the
  triggers are already wired up. This screen only controls the **words**.
</Note>

## What gets sent, and when

**To your customers**

| Email | Sent when |
| --- | --- |
| **Order received** | The moment a COD order is placed. |
| **Order confirmed** | You mark an order **Confirmed**. |
| **Order shipped** | You mark an order **Shipped** — includes the tracking number. |
| **Order delivered** | On delivery, with an invitation to review. |
| **Order cancelled** | An order is cancelled. |
| **Abandoned cart** | A basket has sat idle for 24 hours. |
| **Subscriber welcome** | Someone joins your newsletter. |

**About returns**

| Email | Sent when |
| --- | --- |
| **Return requested** | A customer asks to return something. |
| **Return approved** | You approve it — carries the ship-back instructions. |
| **Return rejected** | You reject it. |
| **Refund paid** | You record the UPI refund. |
| **Exchange confirmed** | You settle a return as an exchange. |

**To you, not customers**

| Email | Sent when |
| --- | --- |
| **New-order alert** | An order lands. |
| **New-return alert** | A return is requested. |
| **Daily digest** | Close of day, with the day's numbers. |

## Changing the wording

Pick a template from the list. Depending on the email you can edit its
**subject line**, **heading**, **intro paragraph**, **body paragraph**, **button
label**, **note box** and **total label**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Edit the fields">
    The page tells you at the top whether you have unsaved changes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send test email">
    Sends the email to you, exactly as a customer would receive it. Always do
    this before saving something you've rewritten.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Changes">
    New emails start using your wording immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  **Leave a field blank to use the built-in wording.** The defaults are written
  and tested — only override the ones where your own voice genuinely adds
  something. Blank is not broken.
</Tip>

## Writing these well

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Subject lines">
    Say what happened, not how you feel about it. *"Your order JR-1042 has
    shipped"* gets opened. *"Exciting news from RJ Jewellers!"* gets ignored.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Cash on Delivery reminders">
    Your order confirmation is the right place to remind people to keep the
    exact amount ready for the courier. It saves a genuine number of failed
    deliveries.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Care instructions">
    The delivery email is the best-read email you send. A line about keeping
    artificial jewellery dry, worked into it, prevents complaints weeks later.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Don't overwrite the useful bits">
    Order numbers, totals and tracking numbers are filled in automatically. Keep
    your wording around them — don't write something that leaves no room for the
    detail the customer actually opened the email for.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Warning>
  **Always send yourself a test after editing.** These emails go out
  automatically to real customers from the moment you save. A typo here is a
  typo in every order confirmation until someone notices.
</Warning>
