---
title: "Glossary"
description: "Every bit of jargon the console uses, in plain words."
---

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="AWB (Air Waybill number)">
    The tracking number your courier gives you when you hand over a parcel —
    Delhivery, Blue Dart, India Post and the rest all issue one. It looks
    something like `SR123456789`.

    The console asks for it before you can mark an order **Shipped**, because
    the customer's shipping email includes it so they can track their parcel.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="COD (Cash on Delivery)">
    The customer pays the courier in cash when the parcel arrives, rather than
    paying online. Right now every order on your store is COD.

    This is why cancellations matter more than on a prepaid store — you've paid
    to ship something nobody paid for. The [Customers](/insight/customers) page
    flags anyone who has cancelled before.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="SKU">
    Your own private code for a piece — `JR-NK-001` for a necklace, say.
    Customers never see it. It exists so you can search your catalogue and match
    a listing to the physical item in your storage.

    Pick a pattern and stick to it. Two products must never share an SKU.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="UTR (UPI reference number)">
    The long number your UPI app shows after a successful transfer — roughly
    twelve digits. When you refund a customer, the console asks for it as proof
    the money actually left your account.

    Keep it. It's what settles an argument three months later about whether a
    refund was paid.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Slug">
    The web-address version of a name. A category called "Bridal Sets" gets the
    slug `bridal-sets`, and lives at `yourshop.com/bridal-sets`.

    The console builds these for you. You never type one.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Draft vs Active">
    **Active** products appear on your storefront and can be bought. **Draft**
    products are saved but invisible to customers — half-finished listings,
    pieces awaiting photos, or seasonal items you've pulled for now.

    Draft is how you take something down without deleting it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Primary image">
    The one photo out of a product's several that gets used as its thumbnail in
    listings and search results. You choose it with **Set primary** in the
    product editor.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Pending / Confirmed / Packed / Shipped / Delivered">
    The five stages of an order, in order. **Pending** means it just arrived and
    you haven't accepted it yet. **Confirmed** means you've accepted it.
    **Delivered** is the end.

    **Cancelled** sits outside that line — an order can be cancelled from any
    stage before it's delivered.

    [The full walkthrough →](/selling/orders)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Ticket number (TK-…)">
    The reference the console gives each customer enquiry from your contact
    form, so you and the customer can refer to the same conversation.
    Orders get their own numbers in the `JR-…` format.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Storefront">
    Your public shop — what customers see. When the console says "shown on the
    storefront", it means customers will read it, so write it for them.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Subscriber">
    Someone who gave you their email address to hear about new pieces and
    offers. They have not necessarily bought anything.
    [Subscribers →](/insight/subscribers)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Abandoned cart">
    A customer added things to their basket and left without ordering. Your
    store emails them a reminder a day later, automatically. You don't have to
    do anything — but you can change the wording in [Emails](/setup/emails).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Daily digest">
    A summary email of the day's numbers, sent to you automatically at close of
    day. Its wording is editable in [Emails](/setup/emails).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
