Most problems are one of a handful of things. Work down this page before
calling anyone.

## First, try these three

<Steps>
  <Step title="Refresh the page">
    Genuinely fixes a surprising share of oddities.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your internet">
    Open any other website. If that's slow too, it's your connection, not the
    store.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try a different browser or your phone">
    If it works there, the problem is with the one browser — clear its cache or
    just use the other one for now.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Common problems

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I can't sign in">
    Check the email is the one you were given, and use **Forgot password** on
    the sign-in screen. If the reset email doesn't arrive, check spam.

    If you sign in but get bounced back out, your account exists without admin
    access — another admin can fix that in seconds on the
    [Team page](/setup/team).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A change I made isn't showing on the storefront">
    Did you press **Save Changes**? Settings and product edits don't save
    themselves, and the page warns you when there are unsaved changes.

    If you did save, refresh the storefront page. If a customer says they still
    see the old version, ask them to refresh too.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A product isn't appearing for customers">
    Three usual causes: it's set to **Draft**, its **stock is zero**, or it has
    no category. Check all three in [Products](/catalogue/products).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A customer says their coupon doesn't work">
    Check whether the code is **Active**, whether it has **expired**, whether it
    has hit its **usage limit**, and whether their basket meets the **minimum
    order**. [Coupons →](/catalogue/coupons)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I can't mark an order as Shipped">
    You need the courier's **AWB number** first. Enter it in the courier box on
    the order, then the Shipped button works. [Orders →](/selling/orders)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I moved an order to the wrong status">
    Use the one-step-back control on the order. Note that stepping back from
    Shipped clears the AWB you entered. Delivered and Cancelled can't be
    reversed at all.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I cancelled an order by mistake">
    Cancelling can't be undone. Phone the customer, apologise, and ask them to
    order again. Their stock has already gone back into your catalogue.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I'm not getting notifications">
    Notifications are **per device**. Turn them on for each phone and computer
    separately in [Settings → Notifications](/setup/settings#notifications), and
    use **Send a test** to confirm. Also check the browser's own notification
    permission hasn't been blocked.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A photo won't upload">
    Usually a file that's too large or an unusual format. Try a normal JPG or
    PNG under a few megabytes. Your phone's photo app can shrink it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A spreadsheet import shows errors">
    The preview names the row and column with the problem. The usual causes are
    an edited **ID** column, a typed value where a dropdown was expected, or a
    renamed heading. [Spreadsheet editing →](/catalogue/bulk-editing)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Return photos say 'unavailable'">
    Reload the page. Return photos are stored privately and their viewing links
    expire, so a page left open for hours needs refreshing.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Customers say they aren't getting emails">
    Ask them to check spam first — that's the answer most of the time. Use
    **Send test email** in [Emails](/setup/emails) to confirm sending works at
    all. If your own test doesn't arrive either, that's a technical problem;
    call for help.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## When to call for help

Get technical help if:

- The whole storefront is down for everyone
- Nobody can sign in to the console at all
- Test emails don't arrive even to your own inbox
- Orders are being placed but not appearing in the console
- You're the only admin and you've lost access

## Things that are safe to do

Reassurance, if you're nervous about breaking something:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Clicking around to look" icon="eye">
    Reading screens changes nothing.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Setting a product to Draft" icon="eye-slash">
    Fully reversible. It keeps everything.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Deactivating a coupon" icon="toggle-off">
    One click to turn back on.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Importing a spreadsheet" icon="file-import">
    The preview runs first — nothing changes until you confirm.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

And the ones that can't be taken back: **cancelling an order**, **deleting** a
product, category or coupon, and **rejecting** a return. Those are the four to
slow down on.
