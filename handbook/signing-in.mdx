## Opening the console

Go to your shop's web address and add `/admin` to the end.

```
yourshop.com/admin
```

You'll be asked for the **email address** and **password** you were given when
the store was set up.

<Tip>
  Bookmark the sign-in page on your phone and laptop so you never have to type
  the address. On a phone, use "Add to Home Screen" and it behaves like an app.
</Tip>

## Staying signed in

The console remembers you. You won't have to sign in every day — usually only
after a few weeks, or if you clear your browser data.

To sign out deliberately, use the small sign-out icon at the very bottom of the
left-hand menu, next to your name and email.

<Warning>
  **Sign out on any device that isn't yours.** A shared computer that stays
  signed in gives anyone who sits down full access to your orders and customer
  details.
</Warning>

## Forgotten password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click 'Forgot password' on the sign-in screen">
    Enter the email address you sign in with.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check that email inbox">
    A reset link arrives within a minute or two. Check spam if it hasn't.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the link and set a new password">
    The link works once and expires after a while. If it's gone stale, just
    request another.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The reset email goes **only** to the address on your account. If you no longer
  have access to that inbox, someone else with admin access has to add a new
  account for you from the [Team](/setup/team) page.
</Note>

## "Not authorised" or bounced back to sign-in

Being able to sign in and being allowed into the console are two different
things. If you land back on the sign-in page or see a permission message, your
account exists but hasn't been given admin access.

Anyone who already has access can fix that in seconds from the
[Team page](/setup/team).

## Who should have access

Keep the list short. Every person with access can change prices, cancel orders,
read customer phone numbers, and see revenue.

Give access to people, not to shared logins — the console records who did what,
and that record is worthless if three people share one account. See
[Team](/setup/team).
