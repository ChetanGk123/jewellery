This handbook is for the person who runs **RJ Jewellers** day to day. It explains
every screen in the admin console in plain language — what it's for, what each
button does, and what to do when a customer is waiting on you.

You don't need to know anything technical. If you can use WhatsApp and a
spreadsheet, you can run this store.

## Two websites, one business

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="The storefront" icon="store">
    What your customers see. Products, cart, checkout. Anyone can visit it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="The admin console" icon="lock">
    What you see. Orders, stock, settings. Only people you've given access to
    can open it.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Everything in this handbook is about the **admin console**. You reach it by
adding `/admin` to the end of your shop's web address.

## The console at a glance

Down the left side of the console is a dark maroon menu. Those fourteen links
are the whole system. Grouped by what you'd actually use them for:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Things waiting on you" icon="bell" href="/selling/orders">
    **Orders**, **Returns**, **Messages**, **Reviews**. These four show a
    number badge in the menu when something needs your attention.
  </Card>
  <Card title="What you sell" icon="gem" href="/catalogue/products">
    **Products**, **Categories**, **Coupons & Offers**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="How the business is doing" icon="chart-line" href="/insight/dashboard">
    **Dashboard**, **Analytics**, **Customers**, **Subscribers**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="How the store behaves" icon="gear" href="/setup/settings">
    **Settings**, **Emails**, **Team**. Set these up once, revisit rarely.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  **The badges are your to-do list.** A number next to Orders, Returns,
  Messages or Reviews means that many things are unhandled. An empty menu means
  you're caught up.
</Note>
