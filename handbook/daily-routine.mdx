Most days you don't need to touch fourteen screens. You need to clear four
badges and glance at one number. Here's the loop.

## The morning pass

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Dashboard">
    Look at the four cards across the top: **Orders Today**, **Revenue Today**,
    **Pending Orders**, **Low / Out of Stock**.

    If **Overdue Pending Orders** further down the page has anything in it,
    start there — those are customers who ordered a while ago and are still
    waiting for you to accept.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Clear the Orders badge">
    Every new order arrives as **Pending**. Open each one and move it to
    **Confirmed** — that's you accepting it, and it sends the customer their
    confirmation email.

    Then work anything already Confirmed towards **Packed** and **Shipped**.

    <Info>You'll need the courier's AWB number before an order can be marked Shipped. [How orders work →](/selling/orders)</Info>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Clear the Messages badge">
    These are enquiries from your contact form. Press **Start** when you pick
    one up, call or WhatsApp the customer using the buttons on the card, then
    press **✓ Resolve** and write one line about what you did.

    [How messages work →](/selling/messages)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Clear the Returns badge">
    Return requests need a decision: **Approve return** or **Reject**. Approved
    returns then wait for the item to physically arrive back.

    [How returns work →](/selling/returns)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Clear the Reviews badge">
    New reviews stay hidden from your storefront until you approve them. Read
    each one, then **✓ Approve** or **× Reject**.

    [How reviews work →](/selling/reviews)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Glance at Low Stock Alerts">
    Still on the Dashboard. Anything at zero can't be bought — the storefront
    shows it as out of stock. Restock it in [Products](/catalogue/products), or
    set it to Draft if it's not coming back.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  **Done when the menu is quiet.** No numbers next to Orders, Returns, Messages
  or Reviews means nothing is waiting on you.
</Tip>

## The weekly pass

Once a week, spend a few extra minutes on:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Analytics" icon="chart-line" href="/insight/analytics">
    Which pieces actually sold. Reorder the winners, reprice or retire the ones
    sitting still.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Stock levels" icon="boxes-stacked" href="/catalogue/products">
    Filter the catalogue by **Low stock** and top up before things hit zero.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Customers" icon="users" href="/insight/customers">
    See who's buying repeatedly. Worth a personal WhatsApp when new stock lands.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Coupons" icon="ticket" href="/catalogue/coupons">
    Deactivate finished promotions so nobody uses an offer you've ended.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## The occasional pass

Things you'll touch every few months, not every week:

- **[Settings](/setup/settings)** — shipping charges, return window, the
  announcement banner, festival promo blocks.
- **[Emails](/setup/emails)** — the wording of the messages your store sends
  automatically.
- **[Team](/setup/team)** — adding or removing people who can sign in.
- **[Subscribers](/insight/subscribers)** — export your mailing list before
  sending a festive campaign.
