Anything a customer sends through the contact form on your storefront lands
here as a ticket with its own reference number.

## The three states

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="New" icon="envelope">
    Nobody has picked it up yet. This is what the menu badge counts.
  </Card>
  <Card title="In Progress" icon="spinner">
    You're on it. Marks the ticket so a second person doesn't duplicate your
    call.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Resolved" icon="circle-check">
    Dealt with, and you've recorded what you did.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Use the tabs at the top to switch between them.

## Handling one

<Steps>
  <Step title="Press Start">
    Moves it to In Progress. Do this as you pick it up, not after — it's how
    anyone else looking at the console knows it's covered.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Reply outside the console">
    Each card carries three one-tap buttons:

    - **WhatsApp** — opens the chat with their number already filled in
    - **Call** — dials on a phone
    - **Email** — opens your mail app with their address filled in

    <Note>The console doesn't send replies itself. It hands the conversation to whichever channel suits — which, for most jewellery enquiries, is WhatsApp.</Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Press ✓ Resolve and write what happened">
    A note is required to close a ticket. One sentence about the outcome, not
    the whole conversation:

    > *Sent a replacement clasp by courier; customer confirmed receipt.*

    That note stays on the card, so in three months you can see what was
    actually agreed rather than guessing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Reopening

A resolved ticket has a **Reopen** button. Use it when the same customer comes
back about the same thing — better one ticket with the full history than two
halves.

## Common situations

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="It's spam">
    Press Start, then Resolve with the note "spam". Keeps the badge clean and
    leaves a record.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="It's about an existing order">
    Handle it here, but leave the note on the **order** as well — see
    [Orders](/selling/orders). Whoever packs that parcel reads the order, not
    the message.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="It's a return request">
    Point the customer at the returns option in their account so it enters
    [Returns](/selling/returns) properly, with photos and a record. Handling a
    return purely over WhatsApp leaves you with no evidence trail.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The phone number is wrong or missing">
    Use the email button instead. If neither works, resolve it with a note
    saying you couldn't reach them — there's nothing else to be done.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
