The first screen after you sign in. Every panel is either a number you should
know or a job you should do.

## The four cards

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Orders Today" icon="bag-shopping">
    How many orders came in today, compared with yesterday.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Revenue Today" icon="indian-rupee-sign">
    What those orders are worth, compared with yesterday.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Pending Orders" icon="hourglass">
    Orders you haven't accepted yet. **Needs processing →** takes you straight
    there.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Low / Out of Stock" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    Pieces at five or fewer, or gone entirely. **Restock now →** opens the
    catalogue.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  The comparison is against **yesterday**, so early mornings look bad and
  Sunday evenings look excellent. Judge a day by the seven-day chart below, not
  by the arrow on a card.
</Note>

## Revenue — last 7 days

A week of daily revenue. What matters here is the shape, not any single bar: is
the week climbing, flat, or falling off since your last campaign ended?

## Recent Orders

The latest few orders with their status. A quick way into any of them without
going through the Orders screen.

## Panels that are really to-do lists

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Overdue Pending Orders" icon="clock-rotate-left">
    Orders sitting unaccepted for too long. **Clear this first, every day** —
    each row is a customer wondering if you exist.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Low Stock Alerts" icon="boxes-stacked">
    What to reorder. "Everything is well stocked" means you're fine.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Reviews to Moderate" icon="star">
    Reviews waiting for approval. Nothing here appears on your storefront until
    you act. [Reviews →](/selling/reviews)
  </Card>
  <Card title="New Messages" icon="envelope">
    Unopened enquiries. [Messages →](/selling/messages)
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Panels that are information

<CardGroup cols={2}>

## Reading it in thirty seconds

<Steps>
  <Step title="Is anything overdue?">
    Overdue Pending Orders should be empty. If it isn't, that's your next ten
    minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Is anything about to run out?">
    Low Stock Alerts. Reorder at five, not at zero.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Is the week going the right way?">
    The seven-day chart. Flat after a campaign ended is normal; flat with a
    campaign running means the campaign isn't reaching anyone.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Empty panels are good news. "Everything is well stocked", "No reviews awaiting
  approval", "No new enquiries" all mean you're caught up.
</Tip>
