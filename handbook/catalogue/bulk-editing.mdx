---
title: "Editing in a spreadsheet"
description: "Changing many products, categories or coupons at once with Excel."
---

Editing fifty prices one product at a time is an afternoon. In a spreadsheet
it's ten minutes.

**Products**, **Categories** and **Coupons** each have **Export .xlsx** and
**Import** buttons at the top of their screen. They work identically.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Export .xlsx">
    Downloads everything currently in that section as an Excel file. Open it in
    Excel, Numbers, or Google Sheets.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Edit the sheet">
    Change what you like. Add new rows at the bottom for new items — **leave
    their ID column empty** and the console knows to create them.

    <Warning>Never edit or delete the **ID** column on an existing row. That's how the console recognises which product you're changing. Change an ID and you'll create a duplicate or an error.</Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Import">
    Press **Import** and choose your file. Nothing has changed yet.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Read the preview, then confirm">
    A summary appears: how many rows will be **created**, how many **updated**,
    how many are **unchanged**, and any **errors**.

    If there are errors, the button reads **Fix the sheet first** and won't let
    you proceed. It names the row and column so you can find the problem.

    No errors? The button reads **Import N changes**. Press it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **The preview is a dry run.** Nothing is written to your store until you
  confirm. Import a file, read the numbers, and press Cancel if they look wrong
  — no harm done.
</Note>

## What's in each sheet

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Products">
    ID, Name, SKU, Category, Price (₹), Sale price (₹), Stock, Status, Badge,
    Blurb, Description, Material, Plating options, Details — Plating, Details —
    Stones, Details — Care, Shipping note, Featured, Fresh.

    Read-only columns at the end: Slug, Primary image URL, Image count. Edit
    those and the import ignores you.

    <Note>Photos can't be uploaded through the spreadsheet. Add products in bulk here, then add their images in [Products](/catalogue/products).</Note>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Categories">
    ID, Name, Description, Image URL. Slug, Sort order and Product count are
    read-only.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Coupons">
    ID, Code, Type, Value, Min order (₹), Max discount (₹), Usage limit,
    Expires, Active. The Used count is read-only — you can't rewrite history.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

Columns like **Category**, **Status** and **Active** are dropdowns in the
exported file. Use the dropdown rather than typing; a typo becomes an error at
import.

## What it can and can't do

| Can | Can't |
| --- | --- |
| Change many prices, stock levels or descriptions at once | Upload photos |
| Create new rows (leave ID blank) | Delete anything |
| Fix inconsistent wording across the catalogue | Touch orders, customers or returns |

<Warning>
  **Deleting a row from the sheet does not delete the product.** The import only
  creates and updates. To remove something, set it to **Draft** in
  [Products](/catalogue/products).
</Warning>

## Good habits

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Export first, always" icon="download">
    Even when you only want to add rows. Working on a fresh export means your
    IDs and columns are guaranteed correct.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Keep the exported copy" icon="floppy-disk">
    Save the untouched download before you edit. It's your before-picture if an
    import goes wrong.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Test with two rows" icon="flask">
    First time doing this? Change two rows, import, check the storefront. Then
    do the other forty-eight.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Don't reorder columns" icon="table-columns">
    The import reads columns by their headings. Renaming or removing a heading
    breaks it.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
